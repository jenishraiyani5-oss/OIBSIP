Biography & Life Story

A journey from humble roots in Rameswaram to the peak of scientific excellence and national governance.

1. Early Life and Educational Journey

Dr. Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam was born on October 15, 1931, in the island town of Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu. Growing up in a modest family where his father operated a wooden ferry, young Kalam developed a strong work ethic early in life, selling newspapers after school to help support his family. Driven by a deep curiosity for natural science and mathematics, he pursued a degree in Physics at St. Joseph's College, Tiruchirappalli, before fulfilling his ambition of studying Aerospace Engineering at the Madras Institute of Technology (MIT).

2. Scientific Leadership at ISRO & DRDO

In 1969, Dr. Kalam joined the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) as the Project Director for SLV-III, India’s first indigenous Satellite Launch Vehicle. Under his leadership, the SLV-III successfully placed the Rohini satellite into Earth orbit in 1980, establishing India's presence in space exploration. Later, at the Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO), he directed the Integrated Guided Missile Development Programme, earning the title "Missile Man of India" for developing strategic missiles like Agni and Prithvi. He also served as the Chief Scientific Adviser during the Pokhran-II nuclear tests in 1998.

3. The 11th President of India (2002–2007)

Elected as the 11th President of India in 2002 with wide support across political lines, Dr. Kalam earned the affectionate title of "The People’s President." He brought warmth, humility, and accessibility to Rashtrapati Bhavan. Throughout his tenure, he remained focused on national development, rural empowerment through his PURA initiative, and encouraging young students to take up scientific research and moral leadership.

4. Enduring Legacy as an Educator and Author

Following his presidency, Dr. Kalam devoted his remaining years to teaching, writing, and mentoring youth as a visiting professor at top Indian universities. Author of inspirational books like Wings of Fire, Ignited Minds, and India 2020, he motivated millions of students until his final moments on July 27, 2015, while lecturing at IIM Shillong. His life continues to serve as an enduring example of dedication, simplicity, and service to humanity.
